Books first, and then everything that grows around them.

Greenwood opened in a single room with two thousand donated books. Today it holds more than twelve thousand across six sections, and the reading room is rarely empty.

We believe a library should be easy to enter and hard to leave. That means comfortable chairs, long opening hours on weekends and staff who know where everything is.

Alongside the shelves you will find a digital lab, a children’s corner, a local history archive and a calendar full of events for every age.

Membership is free for residents. Come in, pick up a card and borrow your first book today.
